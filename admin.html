Change your clothes and prices

How it works

  1. Edit
    Change names, prices, colors, photos and stock.
  2. Save changes
    Press the green button at the bottom to confirm your edits.
  3. Preview, then download
    Press Preview home or Preview shop (they unlock after you save), then Download data.js.
  4. Upload
    On GitHub: Add file, Upload files, drop in data.js, Commit. Live in 1 to 2 minutes.
